1. WHAT THIS PROGRAM IS

LaFirma Remux Forge is a REMUX tool. It repackages a video file that is
already on your computer: it converts Dolby Vision metadata from Profile 7 to
Profile 8.1, converts the audio track, and turns image-based subtitles into
text. It does NOT re-encode the picture, does NOT download content from
anywhere, does NOT remove or circumvent any disc copy protection, and does
NOT come with any film.
